# Close and reopen a ticket

**As an** editor, **I want to** close a ticket when the work is done and reopen it if it comes
back, **so that** the ticket list shows what is still open.

## Acceptance criteria

1. An editor can close an open ticket from its detail page; its status shows "closed".
2. An editor can reopen a closed ticket; its status shows "open" again.
3. A closed ticket appears when the ticket list is filtered to "closed" and not under "open".
4. A viewer can open a ticket's detail page but does not see a button to close or reopen it.
